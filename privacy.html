Osmo Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 5, 2026

Osmo is an emotional performance platform for collegiate athletes. Osmo is provided to athletes through annual contracts with university athletic departments. Individual athletes are the primary users of Osmo. Coaches, athletic staff, and university partners may use limited parts of the platform, but they do not receive unrestricted access to an athlete’s personal biometric data.

This Privacy Policy explains how Osmo collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Osmo mobile app, including when you connect your WHOOP wearable device through the WHOOP OAuth connection flow.

Important: Connecting WHOOP to Osmo is optional. Osmo only collects WHOOP data after you give explicit permission during the WHOOP authorization process.

What We Collect

When you connect your WHOOP account, Osmo may collect the following biometric data from the WHOOP API:

  • Recovery score and recovery percentage
  • Heart rate variability, also known as HRV
  • Resting heart rate, also known as RHR
  • Sleep performance score
  • Sleep duration and sleep stage information
  • Daily strain score

Osmo does not currently request workout data or body measurement data from WHOOP.

Osmo also collects information you enter directly into the app, including daily emotional check-ins using the Positive and Negative Affect Schedule, also known as PANAS. These check-ins help you track emotional patterns over time.

How We Use Your Data

Osmo uses your WHOOP data and emotional check-in data to help you better understand your readiness, recovery, and emotional state. Specifically, we use this information to:

  • Generate personalized daily readiness briefings, such as “Ready to Compete,” “Building Back,” or “Recovery Priority”
  • Recommend well-being modules, including breathing, journaling, reflection, and emotional regulation exercises
  • Help you understand your own trends over time
  • Display your own biometric values back to you inside the app
  • Improve Osmo’s recommendations and user experience

Osmo compares your biometric data against your own historical baseline. We do not compare your HRV, recovery, sleep, or strain data against other athletes.

Who Sees Your Data

You can view your own biometric data and emotional check-in information inside the Osmo app.

Coaches and athletic staff do not see your raw biometric values. This means they do not see your exact HRV, resting heart rate, recovery percentage, sleep duration, sleep stages, or strain score.

Coaches and athletic staff may see limited Osmo-generated information, such as readiness labels and aggregate well-being summaries. These summaries are designed to support athlete well-being and team-level planning without exposing sensitive personal biometric values.

Osmo does not sell biometric data. Osmo does not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party advertising platforms. Osmo does not use your raw biometric data for third-party advertising.

WHOOP Access and Consent

Osmo collects WHOOP data only after you approve access through the WHOOP OAuth connection flow. By connecting WHOOP, you give Osmo permission to collect the specific WHOOP data described in this policy.

You can disconnect your WHOOP account from Osmo at any time inside the app. Disconnecting your WHOOP account immediately stops Osmo from collecting new WHOOP data. Disconnecting does not automatically delete data that was already collected before disconnection. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

Data Storage and Security

Osmo uses technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data. WHOOP OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. Application data is stored in Supabase, which uses PostgreSQL. Osmo applies row-level security policies designed to keep each user’s data isolated from other users.

Osmo’s backend runs on Railway cloud infrastructure in the United States. While no system can guarantee perfect security, Osmo is designed to limit access to sensitive information and reduce the risk of unauthorized use, disclosure, or loss.

Your Rights

As an Osmo athlete user, you can:

  • View your own biometric data inside the Osmo app
  • Disconnect your WHOOP account at any time
  • Request deletion of your Osmo data
  • Ask questions about how your data is used

To request deletion or ask a privacy question, contact Osmo using the support email listed below. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

Institutional Use

Osmo is sold to university athletic departments through institutional contracts. Athletic departments agree to data processing terms as part of their agreement with Osmo. These agreements are intended to protect athlete privacy and limit how athlete-related information can be accessed or used.

Even when Osmo is provided through a university or athletic department, the athlete remains the primary user of their personal biometric information.

Children

Osmo is intended for college athletes and is not designed for children under 13. Osmo does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

Osmo may update this Privacy Policy as the product, legal requirements, or data practices change. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and provide notice where appropriate.
