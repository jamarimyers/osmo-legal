# Osmo Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 31, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 31, 2026

## 1. About This Policy

This policy explains how Stateframe Technologies, Inc. ("Stateframe," "we" or "us") collects, uses and shares consumer health data through Osmo. It is written for Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada's consumer health data law and similar state laws.

"Consumer health data" means personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and that identifies your past, present or future physical or mental health status. The [Osmo Privacy Policy](https://legal.osmoperformance.com/privacy/) covers all of Osmo's personal information.

## 2. Health Data We Collect and Why

### What we collect

* **WHOOP data, if you connect WHOOP:** heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, average and maximum heart rate, recovery, sleep duration, sleep performance and sleep stages, WHOOP cycles (each runs from one sleep to the next), day strain, blood oxygen (SpO2), skin temperature, respiratory rate and day energy expenditure.  
* **What you enter in Osmo:** your check-in answers about how you feel, the emotions and situations you log, how you felt before and after an exercise, notes, journal entries and written reflections.  
* **What Osmo works out from that information:** readiness labels (Push today, Train smart today or Recover today), the direction of your HRV trend, a quality label for your sleep, daily briefings, recommendations and alerts.  
* **Records of how you use Osmo's practices:** which exercises and modules you start and complete, your check-in activity and streaks, and app activity records. Even a record that you opened a practice can count as health data under these laws.

### Why we collect and use it

We use consumer health data only to:

* Provide the Osmo features you ask for: showing your data and trends, comparing you with your own history and baseline, and creating your briefings, readiness labels, recommendations and exercises  
* Show limited information to a coach, only if you turn on sharing with that coach (Section 4)  
* Keep Osmo secure and working, including finding and fixing errors  
* Improve Osmo, using records of which features and practices you use, linked to your account  
* Comply with the law, and detect and prevent fraud, security incidents and illegal activity

We do not use consumer health data for advertising, and we do not sell it. Any other research or product development uses only data that has been de-identified so it can no longer reasonably be linked to you, or happens with your consent. We will not try to re-identify de-identified data, and anyone we give it to must agree to the same.

### How it is processed

Osmo stores consumer health data in its database, which Supabase hosts in the United States. Supabase states that it encrypts customer data at rest and in transit. Osmo's server, hosted by Railway, processes it to create your labels, briefings and recommendations.

## 3. Where It Comes From

* From you, when you use Osmo  
* From WHOOP, if you connect your WHOOP account and authorize Osmo  
* From Osmo, which creates labels, briefings and recommendations from the information above  
* From your device, as you use the app

## 4. Health Data We Share and Who Receives It

### Coaches you choose

Nothing is shared with a coach until you turn on sharing with that coach. Once you do, the coach can see your name and email address, whether you checked in today, your check-in streak, the day you were last active, whether your latest summary is up to date, and an alert when a streak of 5 or more days ends.

If you also confirm that labels from your WHOOP data can be shared, the coach can see your readiness label for today and the last 14 days, the direction of your HRV trend, a quality label for your sleep, and an alert when you have been at Recover today for three days in a row.

Coaches also see combined practice results for their team, with no names or individual scores. Coaches never see your raw WHOOP values, journal entries, reflections or check-in answers. The Privacy Policy's [section on what coaches can see](https://legal.osmoperformance.com/privacy/#s7) has the complete list.

### Service providers

These service providers handle consumer health data on our behalf, under contracts that limit them to doing that work for us:

* **Supabase** (database and sign-in) stores it.  
* **Railway** (server hosting) handles it while Osmo's server processes requests from the app.  
* **Sentry** (error monitoring) receives error reports. Osmo removes health values and written text from most reports before they are sent, but some crash reports and other details can still get through. The Privacy Policy's [section on error monitoring](https://legal.osmoperformance.com/privacy/#s6) explains the limits.  
* **Expo** (push notifications) receives the text of each notification, if you allow notifications, and passes it to Apple's or Google's notification service. Notifications do not include WHOOP values, but a reminder can say whether you have finished today's check-in or practice.  
* **Upstash** (background jobs) receives account and WHOOP connection identifiers so it can schedule WHOOP syncs. It does not receive WHOOP health values.

The full list of service providers, and what each one receives, is at [legal.osmoperformance.com/subprocessors](https://legal.osmoperformance.com/subprocessors/).

### Other cases

* When the law requires it, such as a valid subpoena or court order, or when it is needed to detect, prevent or respond to security incidents, fraud or illegal activity  
* If another company buys or merges with Stateframe, to that company, which must keep the promises in this policy  
* When you ask us to

We do not share consumer health data with related companies (affiliates), and we do not sell it. No other company tracks your activity across other apps or websites through Osmo.

## 5. Your Consent and Choices

We collect consumer health data only as needed to provide the features you ask for, such as connecting WHOOP or completing a check-in. Sharing with a coach is off until you turn it on, and labels from your WHOOP data need a separate confirmation. If we want to collect, use or share consumer health data in a way this policy does not describe, we will tell you and ask for your consent first.

You can withdraw your consent at any time:

* Disconnect WHOOP (Profile, then Settings, then WHOOP). Osmo stops receiving WHOOP data right away and deletes the WHOOP records it holds 30 days later. Readiness labels made from your WHOOP data stay until you delete your account.  
* Turn off sharing with a coach.  
* Delete your account (Profile, then Settings, then Delete account).  
* Email us using the contact information in Section 8.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

* Confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and get a copy of it, together with a list of every third party and affiliate we have shared it with and an email address or online way to contact each one  
* See and correct it. Much of it is shown in the app, and you can download your WHOOP records (Profile, then Settings, then WHOOP). To correct anything, email us.  
* Withdraw your consent, or ask us to stop collecting or sharing it  
* Have your consumer health data deleted

**How to make a request.** Email jamari@osmoperformance.com from the email address on your Osmo account and tell us what you are asking for. You can also delete your account yourself in the app. We may need to confirm your identity before we act. Requests are free. We may charge a reasonable fee, or decline, only if a request is clearly unfounded or excessive, or repeats one you have already made.

**When we respond.** We respond without undue delay and within 45 days of receiving your request. If your request is complex, or you have made several, we may extend that once by up to 45 more days, and we will tell you why within the first 45 days.

**Deletion.** When we delete your consumer health data at your request, we tell our service providers and anyone else we have shared it with about your request. Copies in backup systems are deleted within six months after we verify your request. If you live in Nevada, we delete it within 30 days after we verify your request.

**Appeals.** If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision. We will tell you in writing what we did and why within 45 days of receiving your appeal. If we deny your appeal, you can file a complaint with the [Washington State Attorney General](https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint) if you live in Washington, or the [Nevada Attorney General](https://ag.nv.gov/Complaints/CSU_Complaints___FAQ/) if you live in Nevada.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

When we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. Before a material change applies, we will tell you in the app or by email, and ask for your consent first if it affects consumer health data we already hold.
